# Teach a robot to make PB & J Sandwhich

## Assumptions
- We have a fully charged humanoid robot
- We have a countertop with the following items:
    - Butter Knife
    - Jar of PB
    - Jar of Jelly
    - 2 slices of bread laid side by side on a plate


## Steps to Follow
1. Position self in front of items
2. Open the PB
3. Open the jar jelly
4. Grab the knife
5. Use the knife to scoop out some PB
6. Pick up and spread PB over glat surface of the bread
7. Put PB bread back onto plate
8. Use the knife to scoop out some jelly
9. Pick up the bread and spread jelly over the flat surface of the bread
10. Put down the knife
11. Place jelly bread onto PB bread with covered surfces together
12. Cut sandwhih in half with knife
13. Replace the lids on the jars 
14. Put jars and knife away


## Done When
- Sanwhich is constructed and cut in half
- Area must be clear except for the plate and sandwhich
